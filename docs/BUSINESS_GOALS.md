# ParentPresents Business Goals

## Mission

Rebuild ParentPresents as an evergreen organic-search-driven gift recommendation and affiliate commerce business.

ParentPresents is not a wishlist application.

## Primary objective

Recover and grow the valuable search traffic and affiliate revenue generated by the original Parent Presents website.

## Current phase

REBUILD / SEO RECOVERY

Do not prioritize speculative new features while valuable legacy content remains unrecovered.

## Priorities

1. Preserve historical SEO equity.
2. Rebuild high-value legacy content.
3. Restore affiliate monetization.
4. Ensure affiliate products are valid and available.
5. Increase organic search traffic.
6. Increase affiliate outbound clicks.
7. Improve monetization of existing traffic.
8. Improve internal linking.
9. Improve content quality.
10. Create new evergreen content when justified by search opportunity.
11. Improve site reliability, speed, accessibility, and maintainability.

## North-star metrics

- Google organic clicks
- Google organic impressions
- Average search position
- Organic sessions
- Affiliate outbound clicks
- Affiliate CTR
- Affiliate revenue
- Revenue per organic visitor

## Operating principle

Recover proven historical value before pursuing speculative growth.
